Technical Specifications

Minimum Required Capacities

  • Equipment type: Automatic CNC, hydraulic system or servo motor.
  • Capacity (single wire): Diameters from 4 mm to 10 mm.
  • Capacity (double wire): Diameters from 4 mm to 8 mm.
  • Materials: Carbon steel, stainless steel, plain and deformed (ribbed) wire.

Strict Requirements

Operating Voltage

PREFERRED: 220V

Production Speed and Operational Endurance

Ask the Supplier

The supplier must answer each point in writing. These data are critical for evaluating whether the machine can handle actual work shifts.

Production Speed
1

How many stirrups per hour can the machine produce under normal conditions?

Request the figure for a standard stirrup (e.g., 20×20 cm square, 8 mm wire) as a baseline reference.

2

Does speed vary depending on wire diameter or shape complexity?

Request a table or speed range for 4 mm, 8 mm, and 12 mm.

3

What is the maximum pulling speed (meters/minute)?

This figure allows estimation of actual throughput regardless of stirrup type.

Endurance and Work Shift Capacity
4

How many continuous hours can it operate without pauses due to temperature or wear?

Determine whether it can withstand 8, 10, or 12-hour shifts without performance degradation.

5

Does the machine have overheating protection? How does it work?

Verify whether it auto-stops, issues an alert, or has no thermal protection.

6

What maintenance is required during or between shifts to ensure optimal operation?

Lubrication, pin inspection, cleaning, etc. Request the preventive maintenance manual.

Important: Be wary of suppliers who cannot provide concrete figures. Always request the data in stirrups/hour with a specific shape and diameter as reference.

6

Shape and Bending Capabilities

Ask the Supplier

Questions to raise with the supplier during quotation. It is recommended to request answers in writing and, when possible, with a video demonstration.

Show this image to the supplier and ask which shapes the machine can produce.

1

How many different shapes or figures can the machine produce in total?

Request the exact number or the complete catalog of available shapes.

2

Are shapes factory-preset or can the operator program new shapes?

Determine whether the system is closed (fixed shapes) or open (custom programming from the panel).

3

If custom shapes can be created, how is a new shape programmed from the CNC panel?

Request a demonstration or step-by-step video of the programming process.

4

Can the machine produce all shapes shown in the reference image?

Request confirmation shape by shape. If any is not possible, it must be stated in writing.

5

What shapes or configurations can this machine NOT produce?

Demand a clear list of limitations. Be wary of suppliers who claim "it can do everything."

6

What is the minimum and maximum bending angle the machine supports?

Verify the angle range and whether it applies equally to all wire diameters.

7

How many shapes can be stored in the CNC controller memory?

Know the storage capacity to plan operations with multiple references.

Important: Request a video of the machine producing at least 3 different shapes with deformed (ribbed) steel before closing any negotiation.
